Games:

I have made a lot of different games in my free time using the Godot game engine, which are all posted on itch.io. They were all made for game competitions hosted online.

Blok

This game is my most complete one so far. It is a small puzzle platformer and was made in around 2 days. It's based around a simple 'swapping' mechanic that I explore over the rest of the levels. Although it's not very long (and new mechanics could have been included), I still enjoyed making it a lot.
 Visit the website with the game

Mecon

This game is my most recent one. It is a sort of exploration game but is very short. It took around 7 days. This game I wanted to focus more on art and environment as my other game Blok was as minimalist as I could make it, and I think I succeeded in that regard however it was as a result a lot more time consuming to make. There was also a gameplay issue where people couldn't figure out where to go in the second section as the lever was hidden in the dark. I would like to expand on this idea in the future as I feel it has a lot of potential given the right execution.
Visit the website with the game

Spinatus

This game I have spent the most time on, which is about ~20 days. It is a topdown shooter boss rush. I had a lot of fun designing the boss fights themselves however I think the games mechanics are explained poorly and I probably could have tooken a more gradual approach to teaching them to the player. Mechanics like the 'bullet swap' can just confuse the player if it's explained too early as they are just learning how to shoot at the same time.
Currently I am working on a new game that takes the mechanics of Spinatus and expands on them to make a more complete experience. So rather than just bosses, it may have regular enemies and some exploring though I have still not fully decided that. the game will also have the camera follow the player rather than stay static so travel between different rooms is a lot easier to implement.
